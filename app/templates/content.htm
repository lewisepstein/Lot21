Direct Air Capture (DAC) is a technology that removes CO2 directly from the atmosphere. Unlike carbon capture at emission sources, DAC can be deployed anywhere and removes legacy emissions.

Key Technologies: • Solid sorbent systems (like Climeworks) • Liquid solvent systems (like Carbon Engineering) • Electrochemical approaches

Current Status: Several commercial-scale facilities are now operational, with capacity growing rapidly. Costs are decreasing as technology matures.
